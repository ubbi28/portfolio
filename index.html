About

What I actually build

I'm a Laravel developer who builds real operational systems — multi-role platforms with permissions, inventory that tracks stock across warehouses and vans, purchase orders with backorder logic, barcode scanning, and automated SMS/email notifications. Not templates with a coat of paint.

My core stack is Laravel + MySQL, paired with Blade, Bootstrap/Tailwind, and integrations like Twilio, SendGrid, and Zapier when a client's workflow needs to talk to other tools. I work locally on XAMPP, use Spatie for role/permission architecture, and I'm comfortable owning a project from schema design to a client-facing demo video.

Alongside client work, I'm completing a Software Engineering diploma at Aptech, where I'm part of a six-person team building WatchHub, a Flutter + Firebase mobile app — my exposure outside the PHP world.
